An enrollment predictor tool ol is a data-driven operational framework designed to identify factors associated with successful recruitment and potentially able to predict site enrollment performance. Beyond the historical enrollment metrics, the tool integrates operational, and site-specific indicators to identify sites that are likely to perform well and those that may benefit from early support.
The tool evaluates multiple domains across the site timeframe, beginning before site activation. Early indicators of engagement, such as responsiveness to emails and study communications, engagement during site evaluation and initiation visits, georgaphic location , disease prevelance in area, attendance at investigator meetings, training sessions, site call in hour as well as timeliness in completing startup milestones, and the level of enthusiasm demonstrated by the PI and study team, may provide valuable insight into future recruitment performance. Following activation, additional metrics including site team engagement and stability, pre-screening activity, protocol adherence (including retention metrics), competing studies, pre-screening strategies, further refine enrollment predictions.  
By combining these indicators into a dynamic risk assessment, the enrollment predictor tool enables the sponsor/CRO to identify high-potential sites, recognize early red flags of underperformance, and implement targeted enrollment escalation strategies such as additional training, operational support as well as study clinician scientist and study leadership escalations. Finally, this proactive approach facilitates more efficient resource management, accelerates patient recruitment, and enhances the success of the trial(s).
